Hair & Scalp Treatments

Assessment-led options for hair and scalp concerns.

Why is assessment important?

Hair loss can have genetic, hormonal, nutritional, medical or stress-related causes. The pattern and health history should be reviewed before treatment.

Options that may be considered

  • Platelet-rich plasma (PRP)
  • Hair mesotherapy
  • Supportive scalp care
  • Referral for further investigation when needed

Aftercare

Temporary tenderness or redness may occur. Follow instructions about washing, products and return to daily activities.

No guaranteed outcome can be promised; response and session requirements vary.
