What is the treatment?

Botulinum toxin temporarily reduces activity in selected muscles. Planning considers facial anatomy, expression and individual goals.

Areas that may be assessed

  • Forehead and frown lines
  • Crow’s feet
  • Masseter area
  • Migraine-related treatment when medically appropriate
  • Selected excessive sweating concerns

Before and after

Share your medicines, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, medical conditions and previous treatments. Follow the individual aftercare advice provided by the treating professional.

Suitability, dose and injection points require an individual medical assessment.

Questions About Botox and Filler Costs

When asking about costs, clarify the treatment area, the scope of the proposed procedure and whether a follow-up appointment is included. Botox and fillers are different procedures; comparing prices alone does not establish whether a proposed plan is suitable for you. Contact us for current pricing and appointment information.

The Difference Between Botox and Fillers

Botulinum toxin treatments affect muscle activity; dermal fillers address volume and contour. They are different procedures and are not interchangeable. Suitability is determined through an individual assessment.

Information Source: FDA — Dermal Fillers and Botulinum Toxin
