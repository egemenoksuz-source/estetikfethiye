Botulinum Toxin Treatments

Personalised assessment for expression lines and selected muscle activity.

What is the treatment?

Botulinum toxin temporarily reduces activity in selected muscles. Planning considers facial anatomy, expression and individual goals.

Areas that may be assessed

  • Forehead and frown lines
  • Crow’s feet
  • Masseter area
  • Migraine-related treatment when medically appropriate
  • Selected excessive sweating concerns

Before and after

Share your medicines, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, medical conditions and previous treatments. Follow the individual aftercare advice provided by the treating professional.

Suitability, dose and injection points require an individual medical assessment.
