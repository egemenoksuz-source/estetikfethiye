IV Support

IV options considered only after personal medical assessment.

What is IV support?

Fluids or selected ingredients are administered through a vein when considered appropriate. Need, content and dose require professional assessment.

Before treatment

  • Medicines and allergies are reviewed
  • Kidney, liver and heart conditions are considered
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding must be disclosed
  • Tests may be requested when appropriate

During treatment

Tell the attending professional immediately about pain, burning, dizziness, breathing difficulty or any unusual symptom.

IV support does not promise to treat disease, guarantee immunity or provide a certain result.
