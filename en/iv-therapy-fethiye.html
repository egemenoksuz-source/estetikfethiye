What is IV support?

Fluids or selected ingredients are administered through a vein when considered appropriate. Need, content and dose require professional assessment.

Before treatment

  • Medicines and allergies are reviewed
  • Kidney, liver and heart conditions are considered
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding must be disclosed
  • Tests may be requested when appropriate

During treatment

Tell the attending professional immediately about pain, burning, dizziness, breathing difficulty or any unusual symptom.

IV support does not promise to treat disease, guarantee immunity or provide a certain result.

Questions Before Considering IV Support

Knowing the name of an ingredient does not establish that you need it. Ask why a proposed option is being considered, what it contains and what alternatives exist. A decision about treatment follows an individual health assessment.

  • Why is this ingredient being considered?
  • How are the dose and duration determined?
  • How do my medicines and medical history affect planning?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ask about IV support in Fethiye?

Use the WhatsApp link to ask about appointment availability and the consultation process. Mention the area or concern you would like to discuss. Online information is a starting point; a personal assessment is still needed before choosing a procedure.

Does asking for information mean I need this procedure?

No. Reading about a procedure or requesting a consultation does not establish that it is suitable for you. Ask about alternatives, the reason for a proposed plan and its limitations. You can use the consultation to clarify your questions before making a decision.

What should I clarify about follow-up?

Before deciding, ask how the review will be arranged, who to contact with questions and how you will receive individual care instructions. Clarify the scope of the proposed plan and any follow-up appointment when asking about pricing.
