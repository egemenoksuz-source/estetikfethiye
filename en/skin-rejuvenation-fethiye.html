How is a plan created?

Skin type, sensitivity, current concerns, products and expectations are reviewed before selecting an appropriate option.

Options that may be considered

  • Mesotherapy

  • Polynucleotide or salmon DNA-based options where appropriate
  • Personalised supportive skin protocols

Aftercare

Sun protection, gentle skin care and following the specific instructions provided are important after treatment.

The number of sessions and expected response vary between individuals.

Questions for a Skin Rejuvenation Consultation

Explain whether hydration, texture, pores or uneven tone is your main concern. Discuss the skincare products and treatments you have previously used. Ask why a proposed plan was selected and which alternatives may be considered.

  • Which skin concern will be addressed first?
  • How will the stages and follow-up appointments be planned?
  • How will home-care and sun-protection advice be provided?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ask about skin rejuvenation in Fethiye?

Use the WhatsApp link to ask about appointment availability and the consultation process. Mention the area or concern you would like to discuss. Online information is a starting point; a personal assessment is still needed before choosing a procedure.

Does asking for information mean I need this procedure?

No. Reading about a procedure or requesting a consultation does not establish that it is suitable for you. Ask about alternatives, the reason for a proposed plan and its limitations. You can use the consultation to clarify your questions before making a decision.

What should I clarify about follow-up?

Before deciding, ask how the review will be arranged, who to contact with questions and how you will receive individual care instructions. Clarify the scope of the proposed plan and any follow-up appointment when asking about pricing.
