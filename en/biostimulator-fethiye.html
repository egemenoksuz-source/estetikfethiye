What is biostimulator care?

These treatments may be considered within a personalised plan focused on skin quality. Product and technique depend on individual skin structure and suitability.

How is planning carried out?

  • Skin type and concerns are assessed
  • Face, neck, hands or another suitable area is reviewed
  • Expectations and limitations are discussed
  • Product and technique are selected individually

After treatment

Temporary redness, tenderness, swelling or bruising may occur. Sun protection and individual aftercare instructions are important.

Product selection and treatment decisions require an in-person assessment.

Questions About Biostimulator Planning

Options described as biostimulators may have different product and procedure characteristics. Ask which specific option is being considered and why. Fillers, skin rejuvenation and biostimulators should not be treated as the same procedure.

  • Which product or procedure is being considered?
  • How does it differ from fillers or skin rejuvenation?
  • How will review and follow-up be arranged?
