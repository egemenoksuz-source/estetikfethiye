What are dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers may be considered for volume loss, contour support or facial balance. Product, amount and treatment area are selected individually.

Areas that may be assessed

  • Lips
  • Chin and jawline
  • Cheek area
  • Under-eye and selected facial contours

What should I know?

Temporary swelling, tenderness or bruising may occur. Your medical history, medicines, allergies and previous fillers must be discussed before treatment.

Filler injections are medical procedures and should be performed in an appropriate clinical setting by an authorised professional.

Preparing for a Consultation

Prepare the questions you want to ask: Which options will be considered? What alternatives are available? How will follow-up be arranged? Understand the scope of the proposed procedure and aftercare before making a decision.

Questions About Botox and Filler Costs

When asking about costs, clarify the treatment area, the scope of the proposed procedure and whether a follow-up appointment is included. Botox and fillers are different procedures; comparing prices alone does not establish whether a proposed plan is suitable for you. Contact us for current pricing and appointment information.

The Difference Between Botox and Fillers

Botulinum toxin treatments affect muscle activity; dermal fillers address volume and contour. They are different procedures and are not interchangeable. Suitability is determined through an individual assessment.

Information Source: FDA — Dermal Fillers and Botulinum Toxin
