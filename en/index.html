Patient Information

What to know about treatments

This information is general. Suitability and treatment planning require an individual assessment.

Botulinum Toxin Treatments

Botulinum toxin temporarily reduces activity in selected muscles to soften the appearance of expression lines.

Which areas may be assessed?

  • Forehead and frown area
  • Eye area
  • Masseter and selected excessive sweating concerns requiring medical assessment

Before treatment

Medicines, pregnancy or breastfeeding, known conditions and previous treatments must be disclosed.

Dermal Fillers and Facial Contouring

Dermal fillers are considered within a personalised plan for facial proportions, volume loss or contour needs.

Areas assessed

  • Lips
  • Chin and jawline
  • Cheekbones and facial contour

What you should know

Temporary swelling, tenderness or bruising may occur. Treatment should be performed in a medical setting by an authorised healthcare professional.

Hair and Scalp Treatments

Hair loss can have different causes, so the scalp and medical history should be assessed before selecting treatment.

Options that may be considered

  • PRP
  • Hair mesotherapy
  • Scalp care protocols

Important information

Not all hair loss is the same and responses vary. Further investigation may be advised when necessary.

IV Support

IV support should only be planned after reviewing medical history, current medicines and individual needs.

Why is assessment important?

  • Reviewing allergies and medicine interactions
  • Considering kidney, liver and heart conditions
  • Selecting content and dose individually

These treatments do not promise to treat disease or guarantee a result.
